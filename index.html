Local privacy controlsThese actions remove browser/device copies only. “Clear this device” also removes local Diet sign-in state, diagnostics, Copilot session and display preferences. Cloud nutrition data is not deleted.

Deleting the THIEPN Account is a central account action. It cascades through live Diet rows and in-database recovery snapshots. Encrypted disaster-recovery artifacts can persist only until their configured 90-day retention expires and are not an active account data source.
